# Product Brief

**Product:** Music as Medicine  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** In review

## Working concept

Music as Medicine is a guided, music-centered web app that helps people deepen a current or future microdosing practice by connecting an intention with songs, embodied feelings, reflection, ritual, and a rhythm guided by time.

The free experience functions as both a meaningful standalone resource and an introduction to premium practices and the flagship program.

## Core promise

Create a playlist rooted in your microdosing intention, discover the feelings your music is awakening, and turn listening into a personal practice you can return to in rhythm over time.

## Primary audience

People who are actively microdosing or preparing to begin and want to go deeper with their practice.

They may be seeking:

- Greater intentionality
- More consistency
- Emotional connection
- Self-trust
- Creativity
- Reflection and integration
- A sense of ritual
- Support beyond protocols and schedules

## The problem

Many people approach microdosing with an intention but lack a repeatable way to remain connected to it. Their practice may become mechanical, inconsistent, or disconnected from everyday life.

Music is already accessible and emotionally meaningful. The app helps transform it from background listening into an intentional practice.

## The free transformation

The user begins with an intention and finishes with:

- A personal microdosing playlist
- One or more anchor songs explored through memory, partial listening, or full listening
- A clearer understanding of what they want to feel
- A personalized Feeling Map
- An emotional arc for the playlist
- A listening ritual
- The option to schedule a time to return to the ritual
- Journal reflections they can save or export
- One guided meditation
- A possible next step for going deeper

## Product experience

The experience should feel:

- Personal rather than prescriptive
- Guided without being controlling
- Spacious but easy to complete
- Emotionally meaningful without requiring extensive writing
- Grounded in the body
- Useful on both microdosing and non-microdosing days
- Guided by a gentle rhythm rather than experienced as a single isolated session
- Worth returning to as intentions evolve

## Free product

The free version includes:

- One complete playlist journey
- Curated and user-selected songs
- Guided anchor-song exploration
- Theme identification
- A personalized Feeling Map
- A generated listening ritual
- One guided meditation
- An in-app practice calendar
- The ability to schedule the completed ritual
- One follow-up reminder and external-calendar export
- PDF, email, text, and Notes export
- Optional private journal storage
- An invitation to receive additional practices

## Premium product

The premium version supports an ongoing practice through:

- Multiple intentions and playlists
- A growing meditation library
- Multiple facilitator voices
- Personalized ritual recommendations
- Saved history
- Patterns across journeys
- Follow-up reflections
- Voice journaling
- A continuing practice calendar
- Recurring rhythms and reminders
- Push notifications and external-calendar connections
- Seasonal and intention-based collections
- Expanded song discovery
- Premium teachings and audio
- Evolving playlists

## Flagship-program relationship

The app helps users practice independently. The flagship program provides the human guidance, structure, community, accountability, and deeper integration the app cannot provide alone.

The app can qualify prospective participants through explicit questions about:

- Their current experience
- What they are seeking
- Where they feel stuck
- What kind of support they want
- Their readiness for a deeper program

## Community role

The recommendation library begins with songs curated by the product creator.

Users can suggest songs and explain:

- What the song evokes
- What feelings it supports
- Where it belongs in a playlist journey
- Why it may be meaningful for a microdosing practice

Submissions are reviewed before entering the public recommendation library.

Community submission and moderation are planned for a later stage rather than the first commercial release.

Whether an approved contribution displays the contributor's original explanation, an editor-written description, or both remains an open decision.

## Privacy principle

Users control whether their journal entries are retained. Each journey can be:

- Saved privately to the user's account
- Used only during the current session and removed afterward

The Feeling Map is built through human-centered coaching questions and remains editable. The app helps the user recognize and organize their own meaning rather than interpreting them from a distance.

## First-version boundaries

The initial release focuses on intentional listening, reflection, ritual, and establishing a simple rhythm of return.

Deferred features include:

- Built-in music playback
- Dependence on Spotify, Apple Music, or Pandora
- Public user profiles
- Unmoderated community publishing
- Social feeds
- Complex practice tracking
- A large premium-content library
- Native mobile applications

## Early success indicators

The first version is succeeding if users:

- Understand the promise
- Create an account
- Complete the experience
- Feel accurately reflected by the Feeling Map
- Save or export the result
- Return to use the ritual
- Schedule a future practice or reminder
- Return through the calendar or reminder
- Recommend songs
- Opt into continued communication
- Express interest in deeper support

## Confirmed decisions

- Music as Medicine is the working product name.
- The audience includes both active microdosers and people preparing for a future microdosing practice.
- Song recommendations begin with creator curation and later allow moderated community contributions.
- Users choose whether each journal journey is saved.
- Guided meditations can feature both the creator and additional facilitators.
- Rhythm guided by time is a core product principle.
- The technical architecture must support calendars, schedules, reminders, and future external-calendar connections from the beginning.
- Delivery is divided into a validation prototype, first commercial release, and premium platform.
- The first commercial release launches with creator-curated recommendations; community contributions follow later.
- The initial public recommendation library should contain approximately 100 creator-curated songs.
- The first-time journey may take approximately 15–20 minutes, but this is an experience target rather than a promise or a requirement to listen to complete songs.
- The core journey can be completed with one song and one anchor reflection while continuing to encourage a fuller playlist.
- Active and preparing users share one core journey with light, visible stage personalization.
- Scheduling is optional and highly encouraged; the app creates no event or reminder without permission.
- Email access is passwordless and verified before personal reflection through an on-page code or access link.
- A lightweight account can exist without stored journal content, and first name is optional or collected later.
- Session-only users decide at completion whether to retain only the finished playlist and ritual while raw journal responses are removed.

## Open decisions

- How community contributors and their explanations should be presented after approval.
- Whether a session-only user can email a result without temporarily retaining a generated file.
